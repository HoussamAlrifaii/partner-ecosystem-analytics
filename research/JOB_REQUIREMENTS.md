Reviewed 2026-09-10. Convenience sample of seven readable individual job descriptions on Bayt: six UAE and one Lebanon. Geography follows Houssam's Beirut location and regional business exposure; it is an assumption, not a restriction on his search. We sought Data Analyst/BI roles and read technical requirements, rather than treating job-board search-result counts as demand. This is directional evidence, not a representative survey or an estimate of market prevalence. Wording is paraphrased. Missing mention does not mean a skill is unnecessary.

The mix includes internships, early-career and senior benchmarks. Senior years-of-experience or nationality/student eligibility requirements are not requirements Houssam automatically meets. Accessible pages do not confirm that vacancies remain open. Relative posting dates and site close-date metadata were inconsistent, so only the access date is asserted. Expired/Gone and description-poor listings were excluded.

## Priority decisions

SQL is explicitly mentioned in all seven descriptions, sometimes as a desirable skill. Python appears in five, including optional mentions. Power BI appears in six, often as one of several BI options or preferred rather than mandatory. Do not present these counts as '100% of employers require SQL' or '86% require Power BI'.

The clearest priorities for this portfolio are SQL analysis, trustworthy data preparation, a well-modeled Power BI report, business metric reasoning and concise communication. Python automation supports those outputs. Excel is worth demonstrating through independent checks without replacing Power BI. DAX and Power Query are implementation skills needed to make our chosen Power BI report credible; they are not separately frequency-ranked by this sample. Cloud/refresh knowledge comes in deployment. ML and generative AI are excluded from this analyst build.

## Official technical references

- [Microsoft star-schema guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/guidance/star-schema): use dimensions for grouping/filtering and facts at a declared grain.
- [Microsoft CSV parsing reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/powerquery-m/csv-document): implementation reference for our typed Power Query imports.
- [Power BI Publish to web](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/service-publish-to-web): public exposure and tenant/license considerations must be checked at deployment.
